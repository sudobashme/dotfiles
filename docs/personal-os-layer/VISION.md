# Personal OS Layer

> "I feel like it should be one of the most important things we do is build our own os layer."

This project is an attempt to treat my Mac environment with the same level of intentionality, care, and craftsmanship that people bring to custom Arch + Hyprland rices, custom Kali builds, Morphix remasters, and deep Windows customization.

## Core Belief

Your computing environment is not something you merely *use*.  
It is something you *build*, *own*, and *continuously refine*.

Just because I choose to stay on macOS does not mean I have to accept a generic, off-the-shelf experience. The spirit of building your own OS layer — the deep customization, the self-knowledge it creates, the pride in the result — is portable.

## What "Building Your Own OS Layer" Means Here

- **Craftsmanship over convenience**: I am willing to do the hard work if the result feels *mine*.
- **Self-awareness**: The system should be able to inspect itself and tell me meaningful things (not just regurgitate logs).
- **Beauty + Power**: The environment should feel good to look at and use every day. Like a well-made tool.
- **Cohesion**: Shell, editor, window management, deployment, diagnostics, and automation should feel like parts of one system, not a pile of random configs.
- **Pride**: This should be something I can look at in five years and say "yeah... I built that."

## Inspiration

- Custom Linux distributions and remastering (Knoppix → Morphix → custom Kali)
- Modern Wayland rices (especially Hyprland communities)
- The best parts of the Neovim ecosystem (particularly how LazyVim approaches health, configuration, and user experience)
- Old-school power users who treated their machine like an extension of their mind

## Current State (2025)

The existing setup in this repository is the result of many years of evolution:
- Started with heavy Oh My Zsh usage
- Moved through various plugin managers and hand-rolled configurations
- Carries significant technical debt and "I ripped this from somewhere" patterns
- Has a deploy script that works but lacks the same level of care

This project exists to move beyond derivative work into something original and high-signal.

## Non-Goals

- Switching back to Linux full-time (I love my Mac)
- Chasing every new shiny tool for its own sake
- Creating something so complex only I can maintain it

## North Star

To have a personal computing environment on macOS that gives me the same feeling of ownership and quality that the best custom Linux setups give their creators.

Not because it's impressive to others.  
Because building your own layer is one of the highest-leverage things a technical person can do for themselves.

---

*This is not a weekend project. This is infrastructure for how I think and work.*
